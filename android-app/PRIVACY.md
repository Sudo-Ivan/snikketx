# SnikketX Privacy Policy

SnikketX is a fork of the Snikket Android app, which is based on
Conversations. The app connects to an XMPP server that you choose or that
is provided by the person who invited you.

## What the app does with your data

- Your account credentials and messages are stored locally on your device.
- Messages are sent over XMPP, end-to-end encrypted by default with OMEMO
  where supported.
- Media you send is uploaded to the XMPP server you are connected to.
- If you enable crash reporting, anonymized stack traces may be sent to
  help fix bugs. This is off unless you opt in under Settings > Privacy.
- The free build can check your server's portal once a day for a newer
  app version (`/download/android.version`). This sends no personal data.
- The app does not use analytics or advertising SDKs and does not share
  data with third parties beyond the XMPP server you connect to.

## Your server's role

SnikketX connects to whichever Snikket or XMPP server you sign up with.
That server's operator can see your account metadata and any unencrypted
traffic, as with any XMPP service. Consult your server's own privacy
policy for what it retains.
